四方雲集 私隱政策

最後更新：2026年8月4日

四方雲集（「本App」）係一隻俾親友圍枱打台灣麻雀嘅免費遊戲。 我哋只收集令遊戲運作所需嘅最少資料，唔追蹤你、 唔會將你嘅資料賣俾任何人。遊戲頁側邊有一條細廣告欄支持開發， 只會顯示非個人化廣告（詳情見下面「廣告」一節）。

我哋收集啲乜

  • 帳戶識別碼 — 開App時會自動建立一個匿名帳戶 （Firebase Authentication）。如果你選擇用 Google 或 Apple 登入， 我哋會收到該帳戶嘅識別碼、顯示名稱同電郵地址。
  • 暱稱 — 你喺牌枱用嘅顯示名。
  • 牌局紀錄 — 網上對局嘅過程同結果（邊個食糊、 分數等），用嚟顯示牌局紀錄同計分表。
  • 公開番表 — 你選擇「公開」嘅自訂番表，會連同你嘅 暱稱俾其他玩家見到。

廣告

本App用 Google AdMob 顯示廣告，並且只會要求非個人化廣告 （Non-Personalized Ads）——即係廣告內容唔會根據你嘅個人資料揀選。 為咗提供及量度廣告，Google 可能會處理：

  • 裝置識別碼（例如 IDFV）同裝置資訊；
  • IP 地址——用嚟推算大概地區（唔係精確位置） 及防止廣告欺詐；
  • 廣告互動資料（例如廣告有冇顯示、有冇被㩒）；
  • 診斷資料（例如廣告載入嘅錯誤紀錄）。

呢啲資料唔會同你嘅帳戶或者身份掛鈎。本App唔會用 IDFA、 唔會做跨App追蹤，所以亦唔會出現 Apple 嘅「要求追蹤」提示。 喺歐洲經濟區同英國，App會先徵求你嘅同意先至載入廣告。 Google 點樣處理呢啲資料，可以睇 Google 嘅說明。

我哋唔會收集啲乜

冇精確位置、冇通訊錄、冇跨App追蹤，亦唔會將你嘅資料 交俾第三方作推廣用途。遊戲內只可以傳送固定短句，冇自由文字輸入。

資料儲存喺邊

資料儲存喺 Google Firebase（Authentication 及 Cloud Firestore） 同埋我哋喺 Fly.io 香港區嘅遊戲伺服器。傳輸一律加密（HTTPS/WSS）。

刪除帳戶

你可以隨時喺 App 內「設定 → 刪除帳戶」刪除你嘅帳戶。 刪除後你嘅登入資料會即時銷毀。共享牌局紀錄入面嘅名字 會作匿名化處理。

兒童

本App不設真錢賭博，亦唔會刻意向13歲以下兒童收集個人資料。

Privacy Policy (English)

Last updated: 4 August 2026

四方雲集 (Mahjong Four Winds, "the App") is a free mahjong game for friends and family. We collect only the minimum data needed to run the game. We do not track you, and your data is never sold. A slim ad rail on the game page supports development and shows non-personalized ads only (see "Advertising" below).

What we collect

  • Account identifiers — the App signs you in anonymously (Firebase Authentication). If you choose to sign in with Google or Apple, we receive that account's identifier, display name and email address.
  • Nickname — the display name you use at the table.
  • Game records — the course and results of online games (wins, scores), shown in your game history.
  • Published fan tables — custom rule tables you choose to publish are visible to other players with your nickname.

Advertising

The App shows ads via Google AdMob and requests non-personalized ads (NPA) only — ad content is not selected based on your personal data. To serve and measure ads, Google may process:

  • Device identifiers (such as IDFV) and device information;
  • IP address — used to infer an approximate region (not precise location) and to prevent ad fraud;
  • Ad interaction data (whether an ad was shown or tapped);
  • Diagnostics (such as ad-loading error logs).

None of this is linked to your account or identity. The App does not use the IDFA and does not track you across apps, so you will never see Apple's App Tracking Transparency prompt. In the EEA and UK, the App asks for your consent before loading any ads. For how Google handles this data, see Google's disclosure.

What we do NOT collect

No precise location, no contacts, no cross-app tracking, and your data is never shared with third parties for marketing. In-game chat is limited to fixed preset phrases — there is no free-text input.

Where data lives

Data is stored in Google Firebase (Authentication and Cloud Firestore) and on our game server hosted on Fly.io (Hong Kong region). All transport is encrypted (HTTPS/WSS).

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time in the App under Settings → Delete Account. Your sign-in data is destroyed immediately; your name in shared game records is anonymized.

Children

The App contains no real-money gambling and does not knowingly collect personal data from children under 13.

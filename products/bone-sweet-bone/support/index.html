Game support

Bone Sweet Bone support

Need a hand with your little bone brigade?

Email support 中文支援

Contact support@covanlabs.com. Include your device model, operating system, game version, stage or screen, and steps to reproduce the problem. Screenshots help; remove private notifications and sensitive information first. Never send passwords or payment details.

Getting started

Tap Start on the title screen, then Resume or Main Chapter. The opening stage introduces deployment. Complete stages to earn resources and unlock companions and features. Build your team in Deck; summoning and the Evolution Forge become available through progression.

Saved progress

Progress and settings are stored locally. There is no game account or cloud-save service operated by Covan Labs. Deleting the app can remove your progress. Please do not delete it as a first troubleshooting step; we cannot restore your save from our servers.

Summons, purchases and video rewards

Available summons use earned Gems and Summon Seals. Rates & Pool shows the available units and guarantees. Paid packs and rewarded ads marked Coming Soon are disabled in the initial release: they do not take payment or play an ad.

Settings and accessibility

Settings includes English, Traditional Chinese and Simplified Chinese, larger text, reduced motion, and separate music and sound controls. Dialogue auto-advance and battle speed are remembered across sessions.

If the game closes or stops responding

Close and reopen the game, check for updates and restart your device if needed. Report the stage, what happened immediately before the issue, and whether it repeats. An interrupted battle may need to be replayed.

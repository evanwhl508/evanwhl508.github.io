03 / Placement

Room to breathe.

x is the thickness of the left-middle stroke: 70.656 units on the 512-unit master artboard. Leave at least 1x around the visible artwork.

Measure clear space from visible ink, not from the SVG canvas edge. Artwork canvases do not automatically include the full exclusion zone. At a 36px navbar mark artboard, x is approximately 5px.

Master

64px and above for standalone use. The approved horizontal lockup is also validated at a 35–40px lockup artboard height. Keep it at least 160px wide; use a mark-only Micro C below that.

Micro

16–48px for compact UI. The counter and terminal clearance are simplified for recognition. It is a responsive derivative, not a replacement master.

06 / Care

Keep the identity intact.

Do not add gradients or outlines, rearrange internal geometry, alter the confluence, or attach new symbols. Use the official light and monochrome assets when the primary artwork is unsuitable.

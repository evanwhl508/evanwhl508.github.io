Game privacy policy

Claw Mai privacy policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy covers Claw Mai, developed by Ho Lun Wong (Evan Wong), operating as Covan Labs in Hong Kong. For privacy questions or requests, email support@covanlabs.com.

No game account is required. Your progress stays on your device. Version 1.0 has no active advertising. The upcoming version 1.0.1 introduces optional Google rewarded ads with privacy choices. In-app purchases remain unavailable.

Information stored on your device

Claw Mai saves your coins, tickets, doll collection, machine arrangements, theme unlocks, quests, rewards, decorations and settings locally. It also keeps local play statistics, such as attempts, catches, play time and reward history, to support game progress and balancing checks. These records are not uploaded to our servers.

We do not operate an account or cloud-save service for this game. Apple device backups may include app data depending on your settings. Those backups are managed by Apple, not by Covan Labs.

Rewarded advertising in version 1.0.1

You can choose to watch a Google AdMob rewarded ad in the Shop for 3 virtual coins, up to three completed rewards per UTC day. There are no forced ads, banners or purchases. Ad availability depends on your connection, privacy choices and Google's ad supply.

The ad-enabled version uses Google Mobile Ads and Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP). On launch, UMP checks whether a privacy message is required. The game initializes the advertising SDK and requests ads only when UMP permits it. Ads may be loaded before you tap the reward button, so choosing not to watch a video does not by itself prevent all SDK data processing.

Google's SDK may process your IP address (including an approximate location inferred from it), device or app-scoped identifiers, ads shown and interacted with, product interactions such as launches and video views, and crash, performance and other diagnostic information. Google and advertising partners use this information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, maintain security and improve their services. These SDK records are separate from your locally stored doll collection and game progress.

We request non-personalized ads, disable Google's publisher first-party ID feature and set the maximum ad content rating to G. We do not request Apple's tracking permission, provide Google with a player account ID, or request precise location access. Non-personalized advertising still involves data processing, such as contextual ad delivery, measurement and fraud prevention.

Google processes information under its privacy policy; see also how Google uses information from apps that use its services. Processing may take place outside your country, including outside Hong Kong. Google's retention periods and deletion options are described in its policy. We do not hold a copy of Google's individual ad records and cannot delete them through the game's local save.

Ad choices, purchases and analytics

Where required, the Google privacy message lets you consent, decline or manage your choices. Open Settings → Ad privacy to revisit those choices when that option is available. Otherwise, Settings → Privacy policy opens this page. Withdrawing consent may affect ad availability; you can still play using earned and regenerating coins.

Paid coin packs and subscriptions are not available. Items marked “Coming Soon” cannot be purchased. Unity analytics and cloud crash reporting are disabled; Google SDK diagnostics and ad measurement described above are separate. Version 1.0 keeps the ad libraries inactive and offers the daily free gift instead.

Support messages

If you email us, we receive your email address, message and any attachments you choose to send. We use them to answer your request, investigate bugs and improve support. Please share only what is needed; we do not need passwords, payment details or identity documents.

Our email providers process this correspondence on our behalf and may process it outside your country, including outside Hong Kong. We may disclose information when required by applicable law. Support messages are retained only as needed to handle the request, maintain relevant records and meet legal obligations.

Apple services

App Store and TestFlight downloads, feedback and diagnostics shared through Apple are governed by your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy. We may receive feedback or crash information through those services to investigate problems. This is separate from the game’s local save and does not create a cloud backup we can restore.

Your choices and deletion requests

  • Remove local game data. Deleting Claw Mai from your iPhone or iPad removes its local app data. Offloading retains documents and data. Check your Apple backup settings separately. We cannot retrieve deleted progress from our servers.
  • Control what you share. Send only the details and attachments you want us to receive. Review your Apple settings for platform diagnostics and backups.
  • Ask about your support information. Contact support@covanlabs.com to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent us. We may need to verify that a request is yours and may retain records required by law.

Children and personal information

Claw Mai is intended for a general audience and is not specifically directed at children. It does not ask players to create profiles or provide names, dates of birth or contact details. If a parent or guardian believes a child has sent personal information to support, contact us so we can investigate and handle a deletion request.

Visiting this website

These pages do not use advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts the site and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See the Covan Labs website privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement for website and provider handling.

Changes to this policy

We will post updates here with a revised date. Material changes to data collection will be explained in the app where required.

私隱摘要

Claw Mai 無需遊戲帳戶。收藏、金幣、任務、設定及遊玩統計儲存在本機，不會上傳至我們的伺服器；我們沒有提供遊戲雲端存檔。1.0 版本沒有啟用廣告。即將推出的 1.0.1 版本加入自選獎勵廣告：每次完成可得 3 金幣，每日最多 3 次；內購仍未開放。Google 私隱服務會在啟動時檢查所需選項，獲准後才初始化廣告及預載影片；沒有觀看影片不代表 SDK 完全不處理資料。

Google 及廣告合作夥伴可能處理 IP 位址、推算的大概位置、裝置或 App 識別碼、廣告互動及診斷資料，用於廣告投放、衡量、防詐騙及服務改善。我們要求非個人化廣告、停用發佈商第一方 ID，並限制廣告內容等級為 G；不會要求 Apple 追蹤權限或精確位置。非個人化廣告仍涉及資料處理。如適用，可在 Google 私隱訊息選擇同意、拒絕或管理選項，並於「設定 → 廣告私隱」更改。拒絕或撤回同意不會阻止正常遊戲，但可能影響廣告供應。資料可能在香港以外處理，詳情及保留／刪除選項見上方 Google 政策。

如你透過電郵聯絡我們，我們會收到你的電郵地址、訊息及自願提供的附件，用於處理支援及相關問題。Apple 的備份、TestFlight 意見及診斷資料，按你的 Apple 設定及其私隱政策處理。本網站由 GitHub Pages 託管，服務供應商會為保安用途記錄訪客 IP 位址。

刪除遊戲會移除本機進度，「卸載 App」則保留文件及資料；請另行檢查備份設定。如需查閱、更正或刪除你提供給我們的支援資料，請聯絡 support@covanlabs.com。

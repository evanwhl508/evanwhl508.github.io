Claw Mai

A little claw. A lot of Siu Mai.

Still steaming. Take me home! Line up your grab, lower the claw and try to bring a little Siu Mai friend over the prize chute. Every pile is different, and a careful catch is part of the fun.

Game support Privacy policy

Your own little dim sum arcade

Start in Teahouse Originals and complete quests to open more machines. Choose your next stop: Fantasy Fellowship, Everyday Heroes or Playtime Carnival. Unlock all three to visit the rotating Dim Sum Showcase.

  • 16 dolls to collect. Meet eight Siu Mai characters, each with a full-size doll and a cute little head version. Build matching pairs and grow your collection.
  • A claw with a mind of its own. Check the depth, position your grab and work with the swing. Dolls can tumble, slip and jostle; touching one does not guarantee a catch.
  • A fresh arrangement. Give the machine a shake when the pile needs a nudge. An occasional visiting cat also wanders in to bat the dolls around while you keep playing.
  • More than the next catch. Follow daily quests, collect special items, earn tickets and decorate your own little teahouse.

A few tries, then one more

Your first three attempts are free. After that, one coin starts a round. Earn coins through play and rewards, let them replenish over time, or collect the daily three-coin gift in the Shop.

Single-player gameplay with no account required. Progress is saved on your device. English and Traditional Chinese are available in the game.

Claw Mai is preparing for its App Store release. The launch version has no active ads, in-app purchases or subscriptions. Features marked “Coming Soon” are unavailable. Coins, tickets and prizes are virtual game items with no cash value.

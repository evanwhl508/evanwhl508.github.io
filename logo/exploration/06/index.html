Hidden COVAN geometry

Covan Ligature

One name is built from shared curves, counters and a single connecting stroke.

Same contours. Revealed.

The highlighted paths are the actual filled components used in this SVG. Faint paths show their relationship; no extra letters have been drawn over the mark.

  1. O: the rounded upper counter
  2. V / N: the central interval and diagonal
  3. C / A are suggestions, not five forced glyphs

First impression

A rounded ligature: two uprights joined by an oblique passage.

Why the shape works

The curved shoulder tempers the diagonal. A firm baseline and open lower intervals give the compact form a stable rhythm.

What to discover

The rounded counter supplies an O association; the diagonal and uprights supply N. A V-like interval appears between the uprights. C and A are not independently encoded.

Why Covan

The construction studies the relationships inside COVAN rather than stacking five initials. O, V and N provide a discoverable subset of the name.

Could it belong to another company?

This could suit another name sharing those letters. It is a partial Covan ligature, not a claim that five letters make a symbol impossible to reuse.

What the aqua means

The small upper join identifies the meeting between the rounded framework and the connecting stroke.

Letter-reading limits

O / V / N suggested. C / A omitted to protect the silhouette.

Watch in review

An n-like first reading is likely. The central counter must remain open at 16px; all-five-letter completeness is deliberately sacrificed.

Design assessment

Subjective 1–10 judgments for discussion, not test results or a ranking. “Ownability” describes perceived visual potential; no trademark or similarity clearance is claimed. There is no total or selected winner.

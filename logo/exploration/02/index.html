The highlighted paths are the actual filled components used in this SVG. Faint paths show their relationship; no extra letters have been drawn over the mark.

  1. A broad enclosing gesture
  2. A second gesture turns inside it
  3. Aqua: where the second origin takes shape

First impression

A quiet nested form: a broad upper shelter and a shorter, turned inner gesture.

Why the shape works

The two radii and unequal heights make a balanced, off-centre silhouette. A clear internal channel lets the forms breathe.

What to discover

One origin defines the outside; the second turns inside it. Together they establish a shared space without becoming matching halves.

Why Covan

Covan is a new name with room for both Cobie and Evan. The outside/inside relation describes co-authorship, not a corporate hierarchy.

Could it belong to another company?

Enclosure is a transferable idea. The deliberate two-gesture reading supports the name story, but this direction relies more on repeated brand use than on a literal letter signature.

What the aqua means

Aqua follows the inner turn: the point where the second gesture begins to complete the first.

Letter-reading limits

The shapes are not intended as a readable CE monogram.

Watch in review

Some viewers may see a lowercase letter pair. The inner channel is the detail to compare at 16px.

Design assessment

Subjective 1–10 judgments for discussion, not test results or a ranking. “Ownability” describes perceived visual potential; no trademark or similarity clearance is claimed. There is no total or selected winner.

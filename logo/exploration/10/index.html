Product iteration / Covan loop

Revision Fold

At Covan, what a product teaches us becomes part of its next version.

Same contours. Revealed.

The highlighted paths are the actual filled components used in this SVG. Faint paths show their relationship; no extra letters have been drawn over the mark.

  1. The outer sweep is one pass through a product
  2. The return folds into the same work, rather than circling outside it
  3. Aqua: learning changing the next version

First impression

An uneven loop with a compact return folded into its interior.

Why the shape works

A generous outer arc and a smaller inner curve create two scales of movement. The offset opening keeps it from becoming a regular ring.

What to discover

The second passage returns into the work itself. It represents learning changing the product, not a path going endlessly around it.

Why Covan

The open outer sweep echoes the C/O in Covan, while the internal return expresses an independent lab maintaining and improving its own products.

Could it belong to another company?

Iteration is a shared software principle. The internal return and partial name echo support Covan’s practice, but this direction has a weaker naming claim than the two-origin concepts.

What the aqua means

Aqua is the return where learning enters the next version. It is an integrated curve, not a separate terminal tile.

Letter-reading limits

Only a loose C / O echo is intended. No full monogram is claimed.

Watch in review

A spiral or curled letter may be seen first. The offset fold must remain distinct from a loading-ring silhouette.

Design assessment

Subjective 1–10 judgments for discussion, not test results or a ranking. “Ownability” describes perceived visual potential; no trademark or similarity clearance is claimed. There is no total or selected winner.

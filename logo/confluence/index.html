Does the opening feel recognizably Covan?

The shared idea is a receiving upper curve and a returning lower curve. The four studies test how their tension, curvature and alignment shape one opening. Aqua spans the actual joining point at the back of the C.

Each refinement includes pure white, navy and black proofs; dark and light two-color versions; a construction grid; separate source ribbons; native size tests; and the alternate origin-color treatment.

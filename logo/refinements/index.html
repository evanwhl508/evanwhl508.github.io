Reading the studies

A preserves the clearest modular breaks. B adds a shallow negative-space detail that should disappear before the C does. C prioritizes continuity, with finer seams that visually merge at the smallest sizes.

All three use concentric circular contours, an open right-hand counter, three filled paths and an aqua terminal below the original mark’s aqua proportion. There is no inward bar that turns the silhouette into a G.

Direction 02’s literal aqua V and Direction 03 are discarded. No refined variant has been promoted to the website identity.

The shared construction

Two curved ribbons meet at a common outer tangent. Their offset inner arcs create a single cusp in the counter. The final C is one uninterrupted silhouette, with the joining edge removed.

The five studies vary curvature, opening, counter depth and asymmetry. The wordmark stays the same throughout.

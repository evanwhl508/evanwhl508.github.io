Why does my day lock at 4:00 AM?

A BrewPoo "food day" runs from 4:00 AM to 3:59 AM, so late-night snacks count toward the day you actually ate them. When the day locks, your buddy is finalized and joins your collection — no rerolls, that's the fun.

Why didn't my buddy change when I added a food?

Calories-only entries count for your diary but don't shape your buddy. Add nutrition details (or pick a curated food) to feed it.

Why is a day showing a sleeping buddy?

Days with no eligible food entries finalize as a sleeping placeholder. It stays that way even if you edit that day later — your diary updates, but locked buddies never change.

Where is my data?

Entirely on your device. There's no account and no cloud. You can export everything or erase everything from Settings. See the Privacy Policy.

Is BrewPoo medical advice?

No. The buddy is a playful visualization of your food log, and nutrition figures are estimates. For real digestive or dietary concerns, talk to a professional.

Privacy Policy

Effective July 5, 2026

The short version: BrewPoo stores everything on your device. We don't collect, transmit, sell, or even see your data. There are no accounts, no analytics, and no third-party tracking.

What BrewPoo stores

BrewPoo keeps your food diary, water log, weight entries, settings, and generated daily characters ("buddies") in local storage on your device only. This data never leaves your device through the app.

What BrewPoo does not do

  • No account or sign-up — we never ask who you are.
  • No servers — the app works fully offline and sends nothing over the network.
  • No analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.
  • No selling or sharing of data with third parties — we have nothing to sell.

Sharing

If you choose to share a daily result card, BrewPoo creates an image containing only your buddy, its title, the fun stats chart, and the date — never your calories, foods, weight, or any personal details. Sharing uses your device's standard share sheet, and where it goes is entirely your choice.

Export and deletion

You can export your data as a file at any time from Settings, and you can permanently erase everything with Settings → Delete all local data. Deleting the app also deletes all of its data.

Health disclaimer

BrewPoo's daily character is a playful visualization based on the food you log. It is not medical advice, diagnosis, or treatment, and calorie/nutrition figures are estimates.

Changes

If a future version changes how data is handled (for example, optional cloud backup), this policy will be updated first and the change will be opt-in.

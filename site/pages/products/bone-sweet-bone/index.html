Home is where the bones are

Defend your moonlit estate with skeletal companions. Choose your team, manage mana and bones, and time your spells as enemies approach.

A gently spooky adventure

Explore 32 stages across four chapters, with illustrated conversations and warm humor. Collect companions, improve their levels and discover evolution through the Forge. Trials and Daily Contracts offer further challenges as they unlock.

Make yourself at home

Play in English, Traditional Chinese or Simplified Chinese. Adjust battle speed, text size, motion, music and sound. No game account is required; progress is saved on your device.

The initial release has no active advertising or in-app purchases. Controls marked Coming Soon are unavailable. Summoning uses resources earned through play.

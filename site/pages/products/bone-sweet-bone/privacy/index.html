Game privacy policy

Bone Sweet Bone privacy policy

Last updated: 4 November 2026

This policy covers Bone Sweet Bone, developed by Ho Lun Wong (Evan Wong), operating as Covan Labs in Hong Kong. Questions and requests: support@covanlabs.com.

No game account is required. Your game progress stays on your device. The initial release has no active advertising, in-app purchases or remote gameplay analytics service.

Local game information

The game stores progress, companions, levels, shards, currencies, summon history, rewards, chapter results, story progress and settings on your device. A locally generated player identifier and gameplay records support saving and game logic; they are not sent to a Covan Labs server. Gameplay events used by the local telemetry implementation are handled on-device, not uploaded to a remote analytics provider.

We do not operate an account or cloud-save service. Apple device backups may include game data depending on your settings; Apple manages those backups, not Covan Labs.

Advertising, purchases and tracking

The initial release does not request ads, initiate purchases or offer subscriptions. Related controls marked Coming Soon are disabled. Summoning uses earned game resources. Advertising and payment SDKs are excluded from this release; Unity Analytics and cloud crash reporting are disabled. We do not use gameplay information for cross-app advertising or sell it.

If a future update enables advertising, purchases or remote analytics, we will update this policy and provide any required notices and choices before enabling the new collection.

Support correspondence

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate problems and maintain support records. Share only what is needed; do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents.

Email providers process correspondence for us and may process it outside your country, including outside Hong Kong. We retain support information only as needed to handle the request, maintain relevant records and meet legal obligations. We may disclose information when required by applicable law.

Apple services

Downloads, platform diagnostics, TestFlight feedback and backups are governed by your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy. We may receive feedback or crash reports through Apple to investigate issues. This does not provide a game cloud save we can restore.

Your choices

  • Local data: deleting the app removes its local data; offloading retains documents and data. Check Apple backup settings separately. We cannot retrieve deleted progress from our servers.
  • Support information: email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent. We may verify the request and retain records required by law.
  • Sharing: send only the details and attachments you want us to receive. Review Apple settings for diagnostics and backups.

Children

The game does not ask for names, dates of birth or contact details. If a parent or guardian believes a child sent personal information to support, please contact us to investigate and handle a deletion request.

This website

These pages do not use advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See our website privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Policy updates

Updates appear here with a revised date. Material changes to data collection will be explained in the app where required.

私隱摘要

Bone Sweet Bone 無需遊戲帳戶。遊戲進度、資源、召喚紀錄及設定儲存在本機，不會上傳至我們的伺服器。首發版本沒有啟用廣告、內購或遠端遊戲分析服務。我們不提供遊戲雲端存檔；刪除遊戲可能令進度遺失。

如你電郵聯絡我們，我們會收到你的電郵地址、訊息及自願提供的附件，用於支援及調查問題。Apple 的診斷、意見及備份按你的 Apple 設定處理。本網站由 GitHub Pages 託管，供應商會為保安用途記錄 IP 位址。如需查閱、更正或刪除支援資料，請聯絡 support@covanlabs.com。

隐私摘要

游戏无需账户，进度、资源、召唤记录和设置保存在本机，不会上传至我们的服务器。首发版本未启用广告、内购或远程游戏分析服务。我们不提供云存档，删除游戏可能导致进度丢失。

通过邮件联系时，我们会收到你的邮箱地址、消息及自愿提供的附件，用于支持和问题调查。Apple 服务按你的 Apple 设置处理；网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，服务商会为安全目的记录 IP 地址。有关支持资料的访问、更正或删除请求，请联系 support@covanlabs.com。

Game privacy policy

Bone Sweet Bone privacy policy

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This policy covers Bone Sweet Bone, developed by Ho Lun Wong (Evan Wong), operating as Covan Labs in Hong Kong. Questions and requests: support@covanlabs.com.

No game account is required. Your game progress stays on your device. Version 1.0 has no active ads. The planned 1.0.1 update adds optional rewarded ads for adults through Google AdMob, with privacy choices before ads can load. In-app purchases and remote gameplay analytics remain disabled.

Local game information

The game stores progress, companions, levels, shards, currencies, summon history, rewards, chapter results, story progress and settings on your device. A locally generated player identifier and gameplay records support saving and game logic; they are not sent to a Covan Labs server. Gameplay events used by the local telemetry implementation are handled on-device, not uploaded to a remote analytics provider.

We do not operate an account or cloud-save service. Apple device backups may include game data depending on your settings; Apple manages those backups, not Covan Labs.

Advertising, purchases and tracking

Version 1.0: the initial release does not request ads, initiate purchases or offer subscriptions. Advertising and payment SDKs are excluded from that release.

Version 1.0.1: optional rewarded ads become available in the Shop after stage 1-8. Simply opening the Shop does not initialize advertising or consent services. When you explicitly choose an ad or ad-privacy action, the game asks whether you are under 18 or 18 or older if you have not already answered. It stores this age group locally, not a date of birth. Ads are unavailable for the under-18 group. Adults may be shown Google's User Messaging Platform (UMP) consent or privacy form before the ad SDK can initialize. Availability depends on your privacy choices, region, connection and Google's ad supply. You can continue playing without watching ads.

The game requests non-personalized ads and does not request Apple's tracking permission or access to precise location. Non-personalized does not mean anonymous or that no data is collected. Google's SDKs may process your IP address (including an approximate location), device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, product interactions, crash reports, performance information and other diagnostics. Google uses this information to deliver and measure ads, operate its services and prevent fraud. The SDK's publisher first-party identifier is enabled by default; it is not your game's locally generated player identifier. We do not send your game save, player identifier or summon history to Google.

Google processes this information under its own policies and may process it outside your country. Its retention periods and deletion controls depend on the data and service; deleting the game does not necessarily delete information already received by Google. Read Google's privacy policy, how Google uses information from apps that use its services and Google Mobile Ads SDK data disclosures.

Reward completion and claim records, daily counters and the age-group choice are stored on your device. Rewards are 1,500 coins per completed coin ad (up to two daily) or 20 bonus gems per completed gem ad (up to one daily). The game uses device UTC for daily limits. These records support reliable rewards and do not create a game account or cloud save.

Summoning uses earned game resources. Paid packs marked Coming Soon remain unavailable. Unity Analytics, cloud crash reporting and remote gameplay analytics remain disabled. Future changes to purchases or collection will be explained in an updated policy and any required in-app notices.

Support correspondence

If you contact us, we receive your email address, message and attachments you choose to send. We use them to respond, investigate problems and maintain support records. Share only what is needed; do not send passwords, payment details or identity documents.

Email providers process correspondence for us and may process it outside your country, including outside Hong Kong. We retain support information only as needed to handle the request, maintain relevant records and meet legal obligations. We may disclose information when required by applicable law.

Apple services

Downloads, platform diagnostics, TestFlight feedback and backups are governed by your Apple settings and Apple’s privacy policy. We may receive feedback or crash reports through Apple to investigate issues. This does not provide a game cloud save we can restore.

Your choices

  • Ads in version 1.0.1: watching is optional. Use the game's ad-privacy controls in Settings or the Shop to access Google's privacy options when available or required. Choosing not to consent may limit ad availability; gameplay remains available.
  • Local data: Reset progress clears the game's local progress and related reward/privacy records. Deleting the app removes its local data; offloading retains documents and data. Check Apple backup settings separately. We cannot retrieve deleted progress from our servers.
  • Support information: email us to request access, correction or deletion of information you have sent. We may verify the request and retain records required by law.
  • Sharing: send only the details and attachments you want us to receive. Review Apple settings for diagnostics and backups.

Children

The game does not ask for names, dates of birth or contact details. Version 1.0.1 asks only for the local age group described above and does not initialize advertising for players who select under 18. If a parent or guardian believes a child sent personal information to support, please contact us to investigate and handle a deletion request.

This website

These pages do not use advertising or analytics scripts. GitHub Pages hosts this website and logs visitor IP addresses for security. See our website privacy policy and GitHub’s privacy statement.

Policy updates

Updates appear here with a revised date. Material changes to data collection will be explained in the app where required.

私隱摘要

Bone Sweet Bone 無需遊戲帳戶。進度、資源、召喚紀錄及設定儲存在本機，不會上傳至我們的伺服器。我們不提供遊戲雲端存檔；刪除遊戲可能令進度遺失。1.0 版本沒有啟用廣告。

計劃中的 1.0.1 更新加入成人自選的 Google AdMob 獎勵廣告。單純開啟商店不會啟動廣告或同意服務；主動選擇廣告或廣告私隱功能後，遊戲會先確認本機儲存的「未滿 18 歲／18 歲或以上」組別，不會索取出生日期。未滿 18 歲不會啟動廣告。成人會按需要看到 Google 的同意表格，可在設定或商店使用適用的廣告私隱選項。不看廣告仍可繼續遊戲。

廣告採用非個人化請求，但 Google 仍可能處理 IP 位址及大概位置、裝置或應用程式識別碼、廣告及產品互動、當機、效能及診斷資料，用於廣告、量度及防止詐騙。遊戲不會要求 Apple 追蹤權限或精確位置；SDK 的發佈商第一方識別碼預設啟用。我們不會將遊戲存檔、玩家識別碼或召喚紀錄傳送至 Google。Google 可能在境外處理資料，保留及刪除安排受其政策規管。內購及遠端遊戲分析仍未啟用。

電郵支援會收到你自願提供的電郵地址、訊息及附件，用於支援及調查。Apple 的診斷、意見及備份按你的 Apple 設定處理。本網站由 GitHub Pages 託管，供應商會為保安用途記錄 IP 位址。資料查閱、更正或刪除請求：support@covanlabs.com。

隐私摘要

游戏无需账户，进度、资源、召唤记录和设置保存在本机，不会上传至我们的服务器。我们不提供云存档，删除游戏可能导致进度丢失。1.0 版本未启用广告。

计划中的 1.0.1 更新加入成人自选的 Google AdMob 奖励广告。仅打开商店不会启动广告或同意服务；主动选择广告或广告隐私功能后，游戏会先确认本机保存的「未满 18 岁／18 岁或以上」组别，不索取出生日期。未满 18 岁不会启动广告。成人会按需要看到 Google 同意表格，可在设置或商店使用适用的广告隐私选项。不看广告仍可继续游戏。

非个性化广告仍可能让 Google 处理 IP 地址及大概位置、设备或应用标识符、广告和产品互动、崩溃、性能及诊断数据，用于广告、衡量及防范欺诈。游戏不请求 Apple 跟踪权限或精确位置；SDK 的发布商第一方标识符默认启用。我们不向 Google 发送游戏存档、玩家标识符或召唤记录。Google 可能在境外处理数据，保留和删除安排受其政策约束。内购和远程游戏分析仍未启用。

邮件支持会收到你自愿提供的邮箱地址、消息和附件，用于支持和调查。Apple 服务按你的 Apple 设置处理；网站由 GitHub Pages 托管，服务商会为安全目的记录 IP 地址。有关资料的访问、更正或删除请求，请联系 support@covanlabs.com。

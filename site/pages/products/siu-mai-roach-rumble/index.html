The kitchen is open. The roaches are hungry. Join Siu Mai Jai and his crew, build your defense and stop the invasion before it reaches the plates.

A tiny team with a big job

Combine pea shots, explosive attacks, piercing projectiles and piles of paperwork. Place defenders on the available build spots, choose their targets and upgrade their equipment during battle. Train your team between attempts to make the next defense stronger.

Keep the roaches out

  • 12 campaign stages. Defend winding kitchen paths against runners, flying invaders, egg carriers and the armored Roach King.
  • Three spells. Time your abilities to help the team through a crowded wave.
  • A kitchen that keeps cooking. Earn Kitchen Coins while away, improve your stations and collect daily rewards.
  • More ways to play. Unlock rotating weekly challenges and Endless survival. Choose normal or double speed.
  • Meet the cast. Discover illustrated character stories and learn about enemies in the Monster Book.

Made for your next short break

Single-player gameplay with no account required. Progress and upgrades are saved on your device; leaving an unfinished stage means starting that stage again. The game supports English and Traditional Chinese.

The launch version has no active advertisements or in-app purchases. Controls marked “Coming Soon” are unavailable. The game is preparing for its App Store release.

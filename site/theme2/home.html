04 / Our principles

Care in the details.

A few simple principles guide what we build and how we maintain it.

Useful

Start with a real problem. Give each product a clear reason to exist.

Reliable

Build for everyday use. Make maintenance part of the work.

Simple

Keep the important things easy to find. Make every feature earn its place.

Privacy-aware

Consider data from the start. Explain each product’s practices clearly.
